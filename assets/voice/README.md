# Death voice lines

`death_<n>.wav` plays over an ordinary death, `overkill_<n>.wav` over a death from a
blow bigger than the unit's whole health bar. The two pools are kept apart.

- `death_1`–`death_6`, `overkill_1`–`overkill_3`: the original placeholder lines.
- `death_7`–`death_334`: every *Deadlock* (Valve) hero `pain_death` clip, 328 of them,
  in hero-codename order.
- `overkill_4`–`overkill_78`: every *Deadlock* `pain_akira_laser` clip, 75 of them.

The Deadlock clips are Valve's audio, extracted from Samuel's own install and included
at his decision for a free, non-commercial game. If it is ever sold, or a takedown
asks for it, delete them (and their `.import` files): the game counts the files on
disk, so the pools simply get shorter.
