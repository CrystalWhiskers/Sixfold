# Unit voice lines

Death screams from *Deadlock* (Valve), extracted from Samuel's own install. They are
Valve's audio, not original to Sixfold, included at Samuel's decision for a free,
non-commercial game. If the game is ever sold, or a takedown asks for it, delete this
folder: every unit then falls back to the shared lines in `assets/voice/`.

Files are named `<unit id>_death_<n>.wav` (ordinary deaths) and
`<unit id>_overkill_<n>.wav` (killed by a blow bigger than its whole health bar),
numbered from 1. A unit with lines here speaks them instead of the shared lines.

| Unit | death (Deadlock `pain_death`) | overkill (Deadlock `pain_akira_laser`) |
|---|---|---|
| warden | 6 — Warden | 3 — Warden |
